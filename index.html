Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense and relax different muscle groups to release physical tension:

  • Start with your toes - curl them tightly for 5 seconds, then release
  • Move to your calves - tense for 5 seconds, release
  • Continue up through thighs, abdomen, hands, arms, shoulders, and face
  • Notice the difference between tension and relaxation
  • Breathe deeply throughout the exercise

About MoodMuse

MoodMuse is your personal emotional wellness companion, designed to help you understand, manage, and improve your emotional well-being through a beautiful, calming dark-themed interface.

Our Mission

We believe that emotional wellness deserves the same attention as physical health. MoodMuse provides a safe, private space for self-reflection, learning, and growth. Our tools are designed to help you develop emotional intelligence and build lasting mental wellness habits.

Features

  • Mood-Based Experience: Select your current emotion and receive personalized quotes, music, and activities
  • Emotional Journal: Track your feelings and thoughts in a private, reflective space
  • Wellness Lessons: Learn evidence-based strategies for emotional management
  • Guided Exercises: Practice breathing techniques, meditation, and gratitude
  • Mood Tracking: Visualize emotional patterns and monitor your wellness journey
  • Dark Theme Design: Reduce eye strain and create a calming visual experience

Technologies Used

MoodMuse is built entirely with front-end web technologies:

  • HTML5 for semantic structure and accessibility
  • CSS3 for beautiful dark-themed styling, animations, and responsive design
  • JavaScript for interactive features and dynamic content
  • In-memory data storage for privacy-focused functionality

Privacy & Data

Your privacy matters. All your journal entries, mood logs, and preferences are stored locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers. Your emotional journey is yours alone.

Disclaimer

MoodMuse is a wellness tool designed to support emotional awareness and self-care. It is not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. If you're experiencing severe distress, please reach out to a mental health professional or crisis helpline.
